Murmur

Natural read-aloud and dictation that runs entirely on your Mac.

  1. Start the voice server

    Checking…

    Run this once in Terminal. It installs everything and keeps the server running in the background, including after a restart:

    ~/src/murmur/scripts/install.sh

  2. Connect the extension

    Waiting for the server…

    Automatic pairing didn't work. Paste the token that install.sh printed. It's also stored in ~/Library/Application Support/Murmur/token.

  3. Allow the microphone (for dictation)

    Murmur only listens while you're dictating (⌥⇧D). Audio is transcribed on your Mac and never leaves it.

    If nothing happens, open macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, turn on Dia, then try again.

    Good to know

    • Dense page or busy course site? Press ⌥⇧E for Reader view: clean text, large type and a focus mode.
    • PDFs open in Murmur's viewer automatically. Click Read aloud at the top right. For PDFs saved on your Mac, turn on Allow access to file URLs in Murmur's details on the extensions page.
    • Change speed or voice in the floating player while it's reading. Your choice is remembered.
    • Dictation types wherever your cursor is. Google Docs blocks direct typing, so the text is copied for you to paste with ⌘V.
